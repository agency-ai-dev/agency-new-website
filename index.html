FAQ

Questions Before
You Launch

How quickly can I get started?

Install from the Shopify App Store, answer a short onboarding questionnaire, and Agency AI scrapes your site, builds your brand profile, and has your first campaign ready within 24 hours. No briefing documents, no agency calls.

What platforms does Agency AI support?

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google Ads — managed together as one funnel, not two separate islands. Additional platform integrations are on the roadmap.

How does pricing work?

Founder pricing is locked at $59/mo. Tiered plans launch in 2026 — merchants who lock in now are grandfathered at the founder rate forever, regardless of what plans cost later.

Is my data and ad account information secure?

Your data is used exclusively to run and optimize your campaigns. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties. You can revoke access at any time through Facebook's Business Integrations settings.

Does Agency AI replace my agency?

Agency AI replaces the repetitive execution layer — bid management, creative testing, budget shifting, cross-channel optimization — and surfaces approval-ready actions so you keep control without paying agency overhead for every tweak.

What kind of results can I expect?

Merchants typically see meaningful ROAS improvements within the first 30 days and spend 80%+ less time on ad operations. Results depend on your catalog, budget, and baseline — but the AI is reading signals 24/7 instead of weekly.

Do I maintain control over my campaigns?

Yes. You approve or reject every recommendation before it goes live. Agency AI handles the analysis and execution layer — brand direction, budget limits, and launch decisions stay with you.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — no contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel directly from your Shopify admin. Your campaigns continue running on Meta and Google; Agency AI just stops managing them.
